Scope: read-only review of `fm_stress/analyze_extension.py`, `scripts/build_extension_report.py`, the extension metric definitions and saved results. The numerical training/data/model modules were not changed. The full 96-run array/checkpoint verification is a separate audit; this document does not replace that verification or claim that unfinished cells were inspected.

## Verified analysis logic

- **Source-by-head interaction:** the implemented contrast is `(head GP − MLP GP) − (head white − MLP white)`. Four cells are paired by training seed before bootstrap; context-level contrasts average fixed model seeds and form the paired four-cell difference before resampling. Negative values mean the relative head-versus-MLP score difference is more favorable under GP. They do not by themselves prove superiority in either source cell. Independent scalar recomputation matched all 12 available interactions across the completed synthetic-GP and univariate-ETTh1 datasets and the two forecast metrics.
- **OT-only alignment:** actual ETTh1 data reconstruction confirmed the same 118 chronological test-window starts. The joint model's OT channel is index 6. Targets agree with the univariate targets to a maximum absolute discrepancy of 1.25e-14; training means agree and scales differ only by 5e-14 of floating-point reduction roundoff. The analyzer slices the joint ensemble to `[N,S,F,1]` and recomputes scores on that slice, so it does not compare seven-channel average scores against one-channel OT scores. The same 64 selected origins are used. OT uncertainty currently reports paired-seed contrasts only; the separate full-dataset head/source contrasts provide the moving-block context intervals.
- **Finite-ensemble scores:** independent direct sums over all ordered distinct sample pairs matched marginal fair CRPS and normalized joint energy to 2.23e-16 on nontrivial multivariate test tensors. The correction is `1/(2*S*(S−1))` times the ordered pair-distance sum. Joint energy divides the whole-vector norm score by `sqrt(F*C)`; channel-sum CRPS uses the sum divided by `sqrt(C)`. These normalizations are positive constants within a task, and the metrics' labels match the implementation. They do not make different datasets directly comparable.
- **Aggregation and provenance:** seed SD is computed with sample denominator `n−1`; forecast mean-of-seed scores is not confused with a pooled predictive mixture. Incomplete output is explicitly marked partial and cannot produce final contrasts. The analyzer checks source/data hashes, expected identities, archive shapes and indices, and recorded per-context means. It intentionally delegates independent full saved-array/checkpoint reconstruction to the separate verifier.
- **Baselines:** all baseline scores use the same selected targets as neural models. Validation selects ridge regularization; the residual bootstrap uses whole training residual vectors and is explicitly described as in-sample and potentially underdispersed. Degenerate deterministic forecasts are legitimate proper-score comparators, although they supply no useful interval uncertainty. Lowest observed test means are descriptive minima, not statistically established winners or prospective model selection.

## Statistical limits retained

Three seed values describe optimization variability, including sparse-mask variability, on a fixed dataset/test segment. The paired seed bootstrap has very limited resolution. Real-context circular blocks of four and eight operate on ordered *selected origins*, whose calendar gaps can differ; these are sensitivity analyses and do not establish an ideal block length, stationarity, or population-level significance. Many exploratory comparisons are not multiplicity-adjusted. The OT-only multivariate/univariate comparison also changes output dimension, learned channel mixing, source covariance and capacity allocation, so it is not a pure causal effect of adding auxiliary observations.

Raw velocity labels and their training-fitted PCA bases change with source. Source comparisons therefore use proper forecast scores on common targets; a lower cross-source velocity MSE does not establish forecasting superiority. The reported mean-forecast MSE uses the finite 32-member ensemble mean and includes its Monte Carlo error. Marginal 90% coverage is not simultaneous-path or conditional calibration.

## Observed distinction that the final report must preserve

The completed univariate ETTh1 comparison contains a descriptive source/head crossover. Mean CRPS is **0.14222 for MLP versus 0.17045 for S4 under temporally white source**, and **0.19499 for MLP versus 0.14372 for S4 under GP source**. The S4-versus-MLP difference-of-differences is approximately **−0.07950 CRPS**. This is evidence of source/head interaction under the fixed training budget, not a universal S4 advantage.

Crucially, on that GP-source comparison **S4 has worse raw residual velocity MSE**: **0.039829 versus MLP 0.035686**. Better forecast CRPS does **not** confirm the original claim that S4 resolves the MLP's small-PC velocity error. The source, task, metric and declared budget must accompany any favorable S4 statement.

Baseline context also changes the practical conclusion. In the completed three-channel synthetic GP dataset, dense/S4 neural CRPS means are approximately 0.329–0.333, while the ridge-residual baseline is **0.19294**. On univariate ETTh1, ridge-residual CRPS is **0.09333** and persistence is **0.11692**. Neural architecture ranking alone therefore does not establish usefulness relative to these simpler forecasts. Final reporting must retain all four architectures' completed scores rather than selecting favorable examples.

## Report wording corrections reviewed

Three issues were sent to the report author and confirmed corrected: training targets are allowed in fitted training statistics, so the leakage statement now excludes **validation/test** values rather than all “future” values; the OT page distinguishes its paired-seed intervals from separate full-dataset context intervals; and the report asserts that its current configuration equals the audited analysis configuration. The final author is also adding the observed forecast-versus-small-PC distinction and the strong baseline comparison after completion of all cells.

**Verdict:** no blocking analysis or metric error identified. The report can support a bounded exploratory comparison once the complete independent 96-run verification and final visual checks pass. It should not claim confirmation of the original small-PC hypothesis from the ETTh1 CRPS crossover.
