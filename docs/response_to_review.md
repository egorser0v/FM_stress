## Reporting commitments

Every quantitative claim should identify the experiment, contexts, score units and aggregation unit. Report negative outcomes and baselines alongside favorable controls. Use “consistent across three paired draws” where applicable, not “statistically proved” from a tiny bootstrap. Distinguish test-history uncertainty from uncertainty over retraining and datasets. Later post-hoc diagnostics cannot be described as preregistered primary endpoints.

The final artifact should include a confirmed human contribution statement and an accurate AI-use statement. The present audit does not assign individual human roles or claim that software-agent review is external human peer review. Code, manuscript and slides must use one numeric registry; historical documents may retain earlier scoped figures, but the final account must explain why evaluation sets differ.
