The report and saved audits can be read without the omitted binaries.
Audits that reload models, predictions or training arrays require regeneration
of those artifacts. Follow the training, preparation and diagnostic commands
in output/followup-research/README_RU.txt, using the recorded environment and
seeds. Real-data CSV files are also excluded by the existing repository policy;
obtain the inputs using the project's data instructions. Retraining on another
environment is not guaranteed to reproduce historical hashes bit-for-bit.
The report builder enforces the original artifact audit gates; it is not a
standalone regeneration command for a lightweight checkout.

The research report, manifests and delivery verification are historical snapshots
made before publication. Their statements that no commit/push was created,
and their recorded base revision, refer to that research stage. Publication of
this branch was separately requested afterward; the frozen evidence is retained
without rewriting its hashes or scientific conclusions.
